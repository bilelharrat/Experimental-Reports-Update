How one click turns a company into a bilingual investment memo: eight parallel analyst agents, one pin sheet of facts, and deterministic gates that check every number before the DOCX ships.

Why it got fast为什么这么快

Nothing waits for the whole previous phase. The spine starts on partial results, translation chases each finished section, and a cheap check decides what actually needs redoing.

没有任何环节等待上一阶段全部完成。钉单基于部分结果提前启动，翻译逐个追赶已完成的章节，一次廉价校验决定哪些内容真正需要重做。

The five phases五个阶段

Every agent is a separate claude -p subprocess with the full tool set, including web search. Cost and duration stream back live to the jobs rail.

每个代理都是独立的 claude -p 子进程，拥有包括联网搜索在内的全部工具。成本与耗时实时回传到任务栏。

P1

Prepare准备seconds, no AI数秒，无 AI

Creates the run folder, checks the company is in scope, and stages two durable fact sources for injection: the hand-curated fact_ledger.md and the auto-captured recent_news.md (refreshed from the news tracker before every run). These fix the "fact lottery" — headline facts no longer depend on lucky web retrieval.

创建运行目录、检查公司是否在范围内，并准备两份持久事实来源供注入：人工维护的 fact_ledger.md，以及每次运行前从新闻跟踪器自动刷新的 recent_news.md。这解决了"事实抽奖"问题——头条事实不再依赖运气式的网络检索。

P2

Investigate调查2.3–6.3m

Eight analyst agents run at once, each with its own focus: arithmetic and denominators, time bases, growth bridges, competitive rights, and so on. Each writes a schema-capped JSON artifact the moment it finishes. The phase is gated by its slowest pass.

八个分析代理同时运行，各有专攻：算术与分母、时间基准、增长桥、竞争权利等。每个代理一完成就写出受 schema 限制的 JSON 工件。该阶段耗时取决于最慢的那个代理。

P2.5

The spine — one pin sheet of facts钉单——唯一的事实清单starts early提前启动

One agent distills the passes into the pin sheet: the recommendation sentence, up to 12 key metrics, bear/base/bull scenarios, and 4–6 rated risks. It launches speculatively once 6 of 8 passes are done — but only after the three pin-feeding passes finish, so its numbers can't be built on missing arithmetic. When the last passes land, a ~40-second delta check asks one question: do the late results change any pinned fact? Usually no; if yes, the spine respins and early section drafts are discarded.

一个代理把各项分析提炼成钉单：投资建议句、至多 12 项关键指标、悲观/基准/乐观情景，以及 4–6 项带评分的风险。8 个代理完成 6 个后它就投机性启动——但必须等三个供数代理先完成，确保数字不建立在缺失的算术之上。最后的代理落地后，约 40 秒的增量校验只问一个问题：后到的结果是否改变了任何已钉事实？通常不会；若会，钉单重做，提前起草的章节作废。

P3

Write写作5 sections in parallel5 个章节并行

Five section writers draft the memo at once. The pin sheet is their only fact channel — every writer gets the identical rendered fact block, can cite sources only by id, and cannot add new ones. Seven private analysis artifacts run on a detached thread and never block the memo. Then the acceptance gates, in order:

五个章节写手同时起草研报。钉单是它们唯一的事实通道——每个写手拿到的事实块逐字节相同，只能按编号引用来源，不能新增。七份内部分析工件在独立线程上运行，从不阻塞正文。随后依次通过验收闸门：

  • 1 deterministic structure repair, then renderer validation;
  • 1 确定性结构修复，随后渲染器校验；
  • 2 quality lint on a prerendered DOCX (voice, disclosure, meta rules);
  • 2 对预渲染 DOCX 做质量检查（语态、披露、元信息规则）；
  • pin echo the recommendation must appear verbatim, every metric token somewhere, risk ratings in the risk section, scenario numbers in the financials — checked by code, not by a model;
  • 数字回声 建议句必须逐字出现，每个指标数字必须落地，风险评分要在风险章节，情景数字要在财务章节——由代码而非模型核对；
  • 3 hybrid repair: findings route to the one section (or the envelope) that owns them, repaired in parallel in 1.4–2 minutes — the old whole-package repair took 9.
  • 3 混合修复：问题归位到所属章节（或信封部分），并行修复只需 1.4–2 分钟——旧的整包修复要 9 分钟。
P4

Translate + render翻译 + 渲染overlaps P3与 P3 并行

Chinese doesn't wait for the finished memo: a chaser translates each section the moment it's accepted. Translation sends only the untranslated strings as a numbered list and gets back exactly that many Chinese strings — 56–110 seconds per section instead of 222–398. A fixed style note keeps $24M, multiples, and dates consistent. The final render is pure code: Arial and Microsoft YaHei, roman-numeral sections, sources generated from the envelope. Two DOCX files, about one second.

中文版不等英文定稿：追译器在每个章节验收通过的瞬间开始翻译。翻译只发送未译字符串的编号清单，并按数量收回等量中文——每章 56–110 秒，而不是 222–398 秒。固定的风格说明保证 $24M、倍数、日期写法一致。最终渲染是纯代码：Arial 与微软雅黑、罗马数字章节标题、来源章节由信封数据生成。两份 DOCX，约一秒完成。

Two ways to run it两种运行方式

The pipeline splits cleanly between investigation and writing. Memo Studio parks the run at that seam and hands the pin sheet to you.

管线在"调查"与"写作"之间有一道干净的接缝。Memo Studio 让运行在此处暂停，把钉单交到你手上。

One-Click一键生成

agent decides everything · ~13m代理全权决定 · 约 13 分钟
  1. P1 → P4 straight through, exactly as above.
  2. P1 → P4 一路到底，流程同上。
  3. After it finishes, the studio cards refresh so you can see the agent's rankings.
  4. 完成后工作室卡片同步刷新，展示代理的排序结果。

Studio Review工作室审阅

you approve the facts first你先审定事实
  1. Deep Investigate runs P1–P2 plus one spine, then parks (~9m).
  2. 深度调查运行 P1–P2 加一次钉单生成，然后暂停（约 9 分钟）。
  3. The pin sheet becomes editable cards: re-rank risks, edit ratings, add or drop theses, pick the conclusion stance.
  4. 钉单变成可编辑卡片：调整风险排序、修改评分、增删论点、选定结论立场。
  5. Generate Report composes your cards back into the spine and runs P3–P4 with it pinned (~8m).
  6. 生成报告把你的卡片重新组装为钉单，并以固定钉单运行 P3–P4（约 8 分钟）。
  7. Cards ARE pins.卡片就是钉子。 The same pin-echo gate that checks the agent's numbers now enforces your edits, mechanically. No fallback path can discard them. 核对代理数字的那道"回声"闸门，同样机械地强制执行你的修改。任何降级路径都无法丢弃它们。

News keeps it current新闻让它保持最新

Follow (star) a company and the tracker syncs its news on a loop, fingerprints out duplicates, and classifies each item's impact. Every automatic action is labeled — "regenerated at, affected by" — in the News/Updates tab, on the Overview, and on the report itself.

关注（加星）一家公司后，跟踪器会循环同步其新闻，按指纹去重，并给每条分级。所有自动行为都带标注——"于何时重新生成、受哪条新闻影响"——显示在"新闻/更新"页、公司概览和报告本身上。

low impact低影响

Saved. It enters recent_news.md and quietly informs every future run.

仅保存。写入 recent_news.md，默默供之后的每次运行参考。

medium中影响

Reruns Deep Investigate — fresh cards with the news included.

重新运行深度调查——生成包含该新闻的新卡片。

high高影响

Reruns the full report.

重新生成完整报告。

One guard: while your card review is parked, nothing auto-runs. The recommendation waits with a "Run now" button, and running over a parked review asks for your "I've reviewed the cards" confirmation first.

一道保护：卡片还在待审阅时，任何任务都不会自动运行。建议会带着"立即执行"按钮等待；要越过待审阅状态执行，需先点击"我已审阅卡片"确认。

Speculate on partial input. Verify cheaply. Redo only what the verifier rejects.

用不完整的输入投机推进；用廉价手段校验；只重做被校验推翻的部分。

Every speedup is the same move: the spine before the last passes, the translation before acceptance, the sections before the delta check. It stays safe because the verifiers are deterministic code — the pin-echo check, the exact-match translation merge, the schema caps — not another model's opinion.

每一次提速都是同一招：钉单先于最后的分析代理，翻译先于英文验收，章节先于增量校验。它之所以安全，是因为校验者都是确定性代码——数字回声核对、逐字匹配的译文合并、schema 上限——而不是另一个模型的看法。
